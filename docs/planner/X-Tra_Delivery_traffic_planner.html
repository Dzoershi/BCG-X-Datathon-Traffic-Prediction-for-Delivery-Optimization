Recommended day shift

The brief sets shifts of 4 to 8 hours, paid €10/h by day (08:00 to 20:00) and €14/h at night. The prototype shows the single clearest day shift per day; combining several shifts and drivers is the next step (roadmap, step 3). Badge: how clear the shift is compared with the whole week's daytime traffic.

When should we switch to night?

A night hour costs 40% more (€14 vs €10). Night is cheaper per parcel only if a driver delivers at least 1.4 times more parcels per hour than in the best day shift.
Is the evening (20:00 to 24:00) clearer than the best day shift? Clearer traffic is needed but not enough: the parcels-per-hour gain has to be measured in the pilot.
